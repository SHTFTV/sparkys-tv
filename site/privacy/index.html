Enquiry information

The form prepares an email using the details you enter. It does not send or store them on this website. If you send the email, the recipient receives your email address and the contents you choose to include. Use build@buildershaus.com for questions about your enquiry or requests to remove it.

Purpose

Enquiries are used to respond to your request and understand interest in the planned network. Do not include passwords, payment information or sensitive documents. A contractor handoff requires a separate discussion.

Website services

Hosting providers may process ordinary request information for delivery and security. This version has no membership account, payment checkout or automated newsletter subscription.
