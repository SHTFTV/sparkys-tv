Prepare an EV charging enquiry

Record the property type, parking arrangement, charger location you are considering and whether landlord or strata approval is needed. An appropriately qualified electrical contractor must assess capacity, equipment requirements and installation scope; this website does not make that assessment.

What to include in your enquiry

  • Your city and property type
  • A short description of the project
  • Desired timing and access considerations
  • Questions you want a contractor to answer
