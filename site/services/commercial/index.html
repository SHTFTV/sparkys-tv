Plan a commercial electrical project

Describe the building use, occupied hours, tenant requirements and access arrangements. Prepare available drawings and a list of the equipment or spaces involved. Ask the contractor to explain how disruption, scheduling, approvals and handover documents will be handled.

What to include in your enquiry

  • Your city and property type
  • A short description of the project
  • Desired timing and access considerations
  • Questions you want a contractor to answer
